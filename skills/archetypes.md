# Skill: Archetype Detection & Resume Framing

**Always active.** Run archetype detection on every JD before writing a single resume bullet or summary line. Archetype determines which experience leads, which gets compressed, what the summary angle is, and which bracket tags to use. This is a structural framing step that runs before keyword injection — not a substitute for it.

---

## The Four Archetypes

the candidate's target roles map to four distinct buyer archetypes. Each archetype represents a different mental model the hiring manager has of "the ideal candidate." The same underlying experience gets foregrounded or backgrounded depending on which archetype the role belongs to.

---

### Archetype 1: CRM Strategist

> *"Someone who owns the CRM system and lifecycle strategy end-to-end — not just a campaign executor."*

**Buyer:** Head of CRM, VP Marketing, CMO. They want someone who can think at the system level: define segmentation architecture, set channel roles, own the roadmap, establish measurement frameworks, and influence cross-functional decisions.

**JD detection signals (any 2+ = this archetype):**
- "define CRM strategy", "own the roadmap", "end-to-end lifecycle strategy"
- "CRM governance", "data hygiene", "CRM infrastructure", "operationalise"
- "cross-functional leadership", "connective tissue", "influence without authority"
- "multi-market", "global CRM", "regional CRM"
- "B2B and B2C", "lifecycle frameworks", "journey logic"
- "channel orchestration", "contact strategy", "channel roles"

**Summary angle:**
Open with scope and ownership language: "…with 5 years defining end-to-end CRM strategy, owning multi-market lifecycle roadmaps, and driving customer value through behavioural segmentation and channel orchestration across 8 markets."

**Lead experience (front-load these):**
- Defined CRM and personalized marketing strategy across 8 markets (segmentation, lifecycle journeys, targeting logic, channel roles)
- 7% customer value uplift per user — frame as a strategy outcome, not a campaign metric
- Holdout-group experimentation framework (positions as system-level thinking)
- Cross-functional stakeholder work (co-developed Vendor Funded Deals GTM, QBRs with senior leadership)
- CRM data infrastructure / Braze as a system owned, not just a tool used

**Compress or omit:**
- Individual campaign results
- Tactical execution details (email open rates, push CTR)
- Dawaai detail — reduce to 1 bullet on infrastructure/foundation work

**Bracket tags to use:**
`[CRM Strategy]`, `[Lifecycle Governance]`, `[Segmentation & Architecture]`, `[Cross-Functional Leadership]`, `[Multi-Market Execution]`

**Archetype code:** `CRM-STRAT`

### Archetype 2: Lifecycle Experimenter

> *"Someone who runs the growth engine through structured, measurable testing — scientific rigour applied to marketing."*

**Buyer:** Head of Growth, Growth Product Manager, Analytics-oriented Marketing Lead. They want someone who designs experiments, proves incrementality, and makes decisions based on data — not gut feel or vanity metrics.

**JD detection signals (any 2+ = this archetype):**
- "A/B testing", "incrementality", "holdout groups", "experimentation roadmap"
- "CRO", "conversion rate optimisation", "test-and-learn"
- "attribution", "causal inference", "statistical significance"
- "uplift", "control group", "measurement framework"
- "growth experimentation", "experiment velocity"
- "LTV modelling", "predictive modelling", "propensity model"

**Summary angle:**
Open with methodology: "…with 5 years owning lifecycle experimentation strategy across 8 markets, designing holdout-group tests, proving incrementality, and translating data into CRM roadmap decisions."

**Lead experience (front-load these):**
- Holdout-group experimentation framework — 20% improvement in cost per incremental GMV (frame this first)
- Churn prediction model (7% churn reduction, 11% incremental GMV uplift) — lead with the model methodology
- Incrementality testing for incentive architecture — 4x contamination reduction
- Behavioural prediction model for reactivation — 24% efficiency improvement
- Retention/incentive experiments with holdout groups (improved cost per incremental GMV by up to 20%)

**Compress or omit:**
- Strategy and governance language
- Cross-functional leadership (keep 1 bullet, not multiple)
- Channel execution details
- Loyalty/commercial programme work

**Bracket tags to use:**
`[Experimentation]`, `[Incrementality Testing]`, `[Predictive Modelling]`, `[Holdout Groups]`, `[Data & Measurement]`

**Archetype code:** `LIFECYCLE-EXP`

### Archetype 3: Growth & Lifecycle Marketer

> *"Someone who owns the full user growth funnel — from acquisition and activation through to retention and churn — and can operate at every stage."*

**Buyer:** Growth Lead, User Growth Manager, Head of Retention, or Product-adjacent growth role. They want someone who understands the entire funnel as a system: where users come from, how they activate, why they stay, and what brings them back. This is the "full-stack lifecycle" archetype — no single stage dominates.

**JD detection signals (any 2+ = this archetype):**
- "acquisition", "activation", "retention", "reactivation", "churn" — multiple lifecycle stages in one JD
- "AARRR", "full-funnel", "lifecycle funnel"
- "onboarding", "first order", "first purchase", "D7/D30 retention"
- "freemium", "freemium-to-premium", "subscription conversion"
- "user growth", "growth metrics", "growth loops"
- "LTV", "CAC", "payback period"
- "engagement", "DAU/MAU", "session frequency"

**Summary angle:**
Open with funnel ownership: "…with 5 years owning the full user growth funnel — acquisition, activation, engagement, retention, and reactivation — across consumer tech and marketplace businesses, driving measurable improvements at every lifecycle stage."

**Lead experience (front-load these):**
- Full-funnel framing at Talabat: 7% GMV per user, 3% CVR uplift — present as funnel-level outcomes
- tPro freemium-to-premium: +5pp conversion (activation stage)
- Churn model: 7% churn reduction (retention stage)
- Reactivation: +24% efficiency (reactivation stage)
- PandaPro GTM: 7% penetration in 3 months (acquisition → activation arc)
- Dawaai: +16% visit-to-signup, +30% first-order conversion (activation funnel)

**Compress or omit:**
- Narrow strategy/governance language
- Single-channel execution details
- Commercial/incentive programme work

**Bracket tags to use:**
`[Acquisition & Activation]`, `[Retention & Churn]`, `[Lifecycle Strategy]`, `[Experimentation]`, `[Full-Funnel Growth]`

**Archetype code:** `GROWTH-LIFECYCLE`

**Buyer:** Head of Loyalty, Commercial Director, CRM Programme Lead. They want someone who thinks in terms of programme value proposition, ROI measurement, incentive mechanics, partner relationships, and customer lifetime value — not just campaign execution.

- "customer value", "CLV", "ARPU", "basket size", "visit frequency"
- "roadmap", "programme roadmap", "KPI framework", "performance management"
- "commercial", "commercial strategy", "P&L", "budget"

**Summary angle:**
Open with commercial and programme language: "…with 5 years designing and managing CRM incentive programmes, building ROI measurement frameworks, and driving customer value through data-driven loyalty mechanics across marketplace and subscription businesses."

**Lead experience (front-load these):**
- Incentive architecture redesign — 20% improvement in cost per incremental GMV, 4x contamination reduction (this is programme design at its core)

**Compress or omit:**

- Tactical lifecycle details

`[Incentive Design]`, `[Programme ROI]`, `[Commercial Strategy]`, `[Customer Value]`, `[Loyalty Mechanics]`

### Archetype 5: Growth Strategist

> *"Someone who owns commercial growth programs end-to-end — sets strategy, runs experimentation roadmaps, and drives P&L-accountable outcomes across markets. Uses data to make decisions, but is not a data analyst."*

**Buyer:** Head of Growth Strategy, VP Growth, Commercial Director, or Growth Lead at a consumer tech company. They want someone who can structure ambiguous commercial problems, own programs from strategy through execution, run experiments with rigour, and present to senior leadership — not an analytics specialist, not a channel executor.

**Reference role:** Agoda "Marketing Growth Strategy & Analytics" — the canonical example, though the role identity here is growth ownership, not analytics depth.

**JD detection signals (any 2+ = this archetype):**
- "growth strategy", "commercial strategy", "marketing strategy" with cross-functional or commercial scope
- "P&L", "unit economics", "business impact modeling", "ROI framework"
- "experimentation roadmap", "A/B tests and other measurement approaches" at program level
- "translate business goals / commercial priorities into structured plans / testable hypotheses"
- "pricing strategy", "channel performance", "revenue management" as growth levers
- "executive-ready narratives", "present to senior leadership", "influence senior stakeholders" on commercial outcomes
- "build playbooks", "repeatability and scale across markets / channels / partners"
- "cross-functional" across Finance, Strategy, Product, Supply — commercial program governance
- Data-informed growth language: "data-driven", "surface trends and opportunities", "commercial recommendations from data" — without requiring advanced BI or data science depth

**Summary angle:**
Open with commercial growth ownership: "…with 5 years translating commercial priorities into testable hypotheses and scaling what proves out across 8 markets. P&L-accountable growth programme owner who designs experimentation roadmaps, drives cross-functional commercial programs, and presents performance to senior leadership."

**Lead experience (front-load these):**
- Vendor Funded Deals GTM: EUR 75,000+ Q1 revenue (P&L-accountable commercial programme built from scratch, scaled across 4 markets)
- CPA reduction 15% via A/B testing incentive sequencing cadence across 8 markets (experimentation roadmap ownership)
- 22% increase in trialing customers via first QC-specific PM brief and segmentation model (cross-functional commercial program)
- QC PM Pulse: surfaced that QC had no always-on PM presence; drove first dedicated QC-trial campaign (strategic insight leading to commercial action)
- Holdout-group experimentation framework: 20% improvement in cost per incremental GMV (programme-level measurement rigour)
- VP-level QBRs and budget planning across 8 MENA markets (stakeholder management, commercial ownership)

**Compress or omit:**
- Advanced analytics framing (attribution modelling, composite metric design, predictive modelling) — mention as work done, not as identity
- SQL and tooling depth — mention SQL only in Skills, never as a bullet headline
- Channel-specific execution (email open rates, push CTR, in-app click rates)
- Braze technical depth (Connected Content, Liquid scripting)
- Loyalty/programme gamification design

**Bracket tags to use:**
`[Growth Strategy]`, `[Experimentation Roadmap]`, `[Commercial & P&L Impact]`, `[Cross-Functional Leadership]`, `[Growth Program Design]`, `[Commercial Intelligence]`

**Archetype code:** `GROWTH-STRAT`

## Detection Workflow

### Step 1: Scan the JD for signal clusters

Read the full JD and count detection signals for each archetype. Signals are case-insensitive. A single JD can trigger multiple archetypes — most roles are hybrids.

### Step 2: Rank archetypes by signal count

| Archetype | Signals found | Rank |
|-----------|--------------|------|
| CRM-STRAT | [count] | [1st/2nd/...] |
| LIFECYCLE-EXP | [count] | |
| GROWTH-LIFECYCLE | [count] | |
| LOYALTY-PROG | [count] | |
| GROWTH-STRAT | [count] | |

### Step 3: Assign primary and secondary archetype

- **Primary archetype** (highest signal count): Drives summary angle, which experience leads, which bracket tags dominate.
- **Secondary archetype** (second highest, if ≥2 signals): Contributes 1-2 bullets and secondary bracket tags. De-emphasised but present.
- If only one archetype has ≥2 signals: single-archetype framing.

### Step 4: Apply framing before keyword injection

1. Set the summary angle from the primary archetype
2. Reorder experience bullets: primary archetype's "lead experience" goes to positions 1-2 in each role block
3. Compress bullets from the "compress or omit" list of the primary archetype
4. Apply primary archetype's bracket tags to the leading bullets
5. Inject secondary archetype's framing into bullet positions 3-4 per role
6. Then run normal ATS keyword injection on top

### Step 5: Output the archetype call

At the top of the resume generation output, always state:
```
Archetype detection:
  Primary: [ARCHETYPE-CODE] — [N] signals: [list signals found]
  Secondary: [ARCHETYPE-CODE] — [N] signals: [list signals found]
  Framing: [one sentence on how this shapes the resume]
```
