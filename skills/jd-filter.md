# Skill: JD Filter

**Always active.** Apply whenever evaluating whether a role qualifies for the candidate's pipeline. The title alone is NEVER sufficient — always verify JD content before including or excluding.

---

## Include if the JD mentions ANY of:

**Lifecycle / CRM signals:**
- Lifecycle stages: activation, adoption, retention, reactivation, churn
- Owned channels: email, push notifications, in-app messaging, SMS
- CRM tools: Braze, MoEngage, Iterable, CleverTap, WebEngage
- Experimentation: A/B testing, incrementality testing, holdout groups
- Cohort analysis, user segmentation, LTV, behavioural prediction
- Incentive design, promotion management, voucher strategy
- AARRR framework language (acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue)
- Subscription, freemium-to-premium, membership conversion
- Grocery, retail, new verticals, quick commerce with user-level metrics
- GTM + product adoption (PMM lens)
- Funnel optimisation, conversion rate across lifecycle stages

**Growth Strategy signals (HIGH PRIORITY — include on any 2+):**
- P&L ownership, unit economics, business impact modeling, ROI frameworks in a growth/marketing context
- Experimentation roadmap at program or commercial level: "test-and-learn culture", "testable hypotheses", "scalable frameworks", "measurement approaches"
- "Translate commercial priorities / business goals into structured plans" — strategy-to-execution bridge language
- Pricing strategy, channel performance optimization, revenue management as growth levers
- Cross-functional commercial programs spanning Product, Finance, Strategy, Supply — with P&L accountability
- "Executive-ready narratives", "present to senior leadership", "influence senior stakeholders" on commercial program outcomes
- "Build playbooks", "repeatability and scale across markets / channels / partners"
- Data-informed language: "data-driven decisions", "surface trends and opportunities", "commercial recommendations from data" — role uses data but is not a pure analytics/BI role

## Exclude if the JD is primarily about:
- Paid media, performance marketing, media buying as the core responsibility
- Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads campaign management
- OOH, brand awareness (without lifecycle component)
- Sales, business development, account management
- Partner/co-marketing for a single external brand (e.g. managing Rewe/Tesco partnership)
- Restaurant/partner marketing only
- B2B growth without any consumer lifecycle component
- Indian-headquartered company (hard exclusion regardless of role type)

## Ambiguous titles — always fetch full JD before deciding:
- "Growth Marketing Manager"
- "Growth Manager"
- "Marketing Manager"
- "Senior Marketing Manager"
- "User Growth Manager" — verify consumer-side, not B2B/sales pipeline
- "New Verticals Manager" — verify lifecycle/growth focus, not ops/partner management
- "Marketing Growth Strategy & Analytics", "Growth Strategy & Analytics" — include if JD has P&L/experimentation roadmap/commercial analytics signals (GROWTH-STRAT archetype)
- "Growth Analytics Manager", "Marketing Analytics Manager" — include if growth/commercial program scope; exclude if pure BI reporting role
- "Commercial Growth Manager", "Marketing Strategy Manager" — include if consumer product + cross-functional program ownership

## Language flags — log but don't hard-exclude:
- "Fluent German/French/Dutch required" → flag as risk in Red Flags, still log if otherwise strong (score 70+)
