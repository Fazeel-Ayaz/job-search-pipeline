## Decision Thresholds

- **60+ → Proceed** to resume tailoring + Notion logging
- **45–59 → Log only** (Status = To Apply, no resume generated, flag for manual review)
- **<45 → Skip** (add to scanned_history.json, do not log to Notion)

---

## Score Output Format

```
Score: [X]/100
  Industry:    [X]/20  ([rationale])
  JD Signals:  [X]/20  ([N signals found: list])
  Experience:  [X]/15  ([years comparison])
  Keywords:    [X]/15  ([% match — missing: list])
  Seniority:   [X]/15  ([JD level vs candidate level])
  Location:    [X]/15  ([location/visa situation])

Qualify: YES | NO | BORDERLINE
Missing keywords to inject: [keyword] (Critical), [keyword] (High)
Red flags: [any flags]
```
